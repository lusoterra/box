# Privacy Policy  

**Last updated: 30th of October 2026**

Box does **not** collect any personal data. All configuration data, including credentials for supported booru platforms, is stored **locally on your device**. Your favourites, download history, and search history are also stored locally and are not shared with us.

Please note that third-party booru websites you access through the app may collect data independently. Box has no control over and is not responsible for their data collection practices.
